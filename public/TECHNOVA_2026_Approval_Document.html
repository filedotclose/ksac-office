Proposal for TECHNOVA 2026 — Annual Inter-University Innovation & Technical Conclave
Society Name : KSHITIJ - The Robotics & Innovation Society under KSAC
Event Date : 14th - 15th November 2026  |  Venue : Campus 6 & Campus 7

1. Background :-

TECHNOVA 2026 is designed as Eastern India's flagship technical congregation bringing together over 1,500 student innovators across KIIT and premier technical institutions. The conclave fosters hands-on multidisciplinary engineering culture, indigenous hardware prototyping, and autonomous systems innovation.

Target Audience & Eligibility:

  • Participants: 1st, 2nd, 3rd and 4th Year B.Tech / M.Tech / MCA Students
  • Eligibility: Open to all verified KIIT University students and registered external collegiate teams
  • Team formation: Teams of 3 to 5 participants
  • Advancement / Selection: Top 8 teams from technical inspection advance to final knockouts
  • Safety goggles and Li-Po battery flameproof enclosure bags are strictly mandatory.

2. Objective :-

  • Foster engineering innovation and hardware prototyping among undergraduate students.
  • Showcase autonomous robotics, IoT systems, and AI-driven applications across KIIT schools.
  • Provide a premier competitive arena for national collegiate innovators and robotics enthusiasts.

3. Event Format & Structure :-

A 2-day dual-track technical conclave with combat robotics, autonomous rover obstacle trials, and a 24-hour embedded systems hackathon.

Event 1 — RoboCombat 2026 Arena Championship - Day 1 - 2026-11-14 [09:00 AM - 01:00 PM]

15kg and 30kg combat robotics tournament inside reinforced polycarbonate arena.

Event 2 — Autonomous Rover Challenge - Day 1 - 2026-11-14 [02:00 PM - 06:00 PM]

Vision-based obstacle avoidance and autonomous navigation trial over terrain.

Event 3 — HackGrid 24-Hour Hardware Sprint - Day 2 - 2026-11-15 [09:00 AM - 05:00 PM]

Real-time embedded systems, sensor interfacing, and Edge AI hardware hackathon.
